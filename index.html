It has since become my dream to become a researcher, and I am seeking oportunities that will help me be the best researcher I can be.

Current Research Projects

My current research projects are within the general focus of particle astrophysics. I work primarily with two differetn collaborations, RNO-G and CoRaLS.

My Neutrino Project

RNO-G is a Radio Neutrino Observatory located in Greenland that is aimed to detect ultra-high energy neutrinos using in ice radio antennas. I currently am working on improving the horizontally polarized antenna used in not only RNO-G but other simialr experiments like IceCube and ARA. Using a electromagnetic simulation software called XFdtd I test and optimize different parameters to better understand the phase space of our antennas and improve their sensitivities. I closesly colaborate with California Polytechnic State University and Penn State University to validate my simulations with experimental data.

My Lunar Project

My other research focus currently uses similar simulation techniques for CoRaLS, a Cosmic Ray Lunar Satalite instrument proposal for NASA. CoRaLS is using a novel approach to detect large water deposits under the lunar surface by measuring the radio emissions from cosmic ray interactions with the lunar regolith. I work with Payton Linton on modeling different rock distributions just underneath the lunar surface to predict how the instrument would preform if deployed. To do this I again use XFdtd to create a number of randomly generated distributions and preform different analyses to understand how lunar materials are effected by comsic ray interaction.
